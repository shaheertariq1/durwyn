1. Executive Summary & Purpose

Trouble Cheat is an immediate-response legal protection and personal safety mobile application. It is engineered specifically for high-stress situations—such as traffic stops, unexpected confrontations, late-night transit, or personal emergencies—where rapid communication and objective documentation are critical.

The platform bridges the gap between an active incident and subsequent legal verification. By guaranteeing that exact timestamps, GPS coordinates, communications, and situational details are recorded accurately, Trouble Cheat ensures users have verifiable, tamper-proof records to protect their legal rights and physical safety.

2. Current Implemented Features (Built & Verified in App)

All user interfaces, screen navigation flows, database schemas, and core response mechanisms described below have been developed and tested within the application:

A. Account Setup & Confidential Safe Phrase

  • User Profile: Users establish their profile with verified name, phone number, and account credentials.
  • Confidential Emergency Safe Phrase: Users choose a designated verbal safe phrase (such as "Red Umbrella" or a custom term). This phrase serves as the confidential trigger to activate emergency alerts.
  • Custom Distress Message: Users configure their default emergency broadcast (for example: "I have been pulled over by law enforcement. Here is my live GPS location. Please monitor.").

B. Smart Emergency Contacts & Individual Privacy Controls

Users configure a personal safety network of trusted individuals. To protect personal privacy, sharing permissions are customized individually for each contact:

  • Priority Ordering: Contacts are assigned an alert sequence (Primary, Secondary, etc.) ensuring that immediate family members or legal advisors receive notifications first.
  • Live GPS Permission: Grants or restricts whether that specific contact can view moving coordinates in real time.
  • Audio Recording Access: Controls whether the contact has access to recorded ambient background audio.
  • Medical and Situational Profile Access: Controls access to medical notes, allergies, or vehicle descriptions during an active emergency.

3. Emergency Response Operational Modes

A. Instant SOS Mode

Positioned prominently at the center of the Home screen. Tapping the red SOS button initiates an instantaneous incident. It locks in the user’s exact GPS coordinates, generates a private web tracking link, compiles the pre-set distress message, and sends the alert package to all authorized contacts.

B. Silent Emergency (Stealth Mode)

Engineered specifically for encounters where visible activity on a smartphone could escalate danger or provoke hostility. When triggered, the phone does not emit sirens, vibrate, or flash the screen. The interface remains discreet while silently transmitting live location updates and emergency alerts in the background.

C. Safety Timer ("Walk With Me" Mode)

Designed for transitional risk situations, such as walking to a vehicle late at night or entering an unfamiliar venue. The user sets a timer duration (such as 15 or 30 minutes).

  • If the user arrives safely, they tap "I am Safe", which disarms the countdown.
  • If the countdown reaches zero without user confirmation, the system assumes incapacity or distress and automatically dispatches a full emergency SOS with their last known coordinates to all designated contacts.

D. Safety Monitor (Continuous Guardian Session)

An exclusive Premium tier capability designed for sustained surveillance during travel. The application maintains an active monitoring session that sends periodic telemetry pings, checks battery health, logs moving coordinates, and records breadcrumb points until concluded by the user.

4. Why External APIs & Cloud Infrastructure Were Requested

Currently, the application runs inside our secure development sandbox. To make Trouble Cheat available to real users on mobile app stores, specific external services and infrastructure are required. Below is a plain-English explanation of why each service was requested:

1. Telnyx API (Direct Carrier SMS & Automated Phone Calling)
Why it is needed: Standard push notifications are not reliable in genuine emergencies. Emergency contacts may be asleep, have their phones set to "Do Not Disturb", or may not have the Trouble Cheat app installed.
What it does: Telnyx connects the application directly to cellular telephone networks. The moment an emergency or silent alert is triggered, Telnyx immediately sends carrier-grade SMS text messages and initiates automated phone calls to each contact’s real cell phone number, ensuring the alert is seen immediately.

2. Live Production Google Firebase Account
Why it is needed: During current development, user accounts and database records are kept in a local test emulator.
What it does: Live production Firebase provides the official Google Cloud database, real-time user authentication, and encrypted file storage. This ensures that user data is synchronized in real time across devices with 99.99% uptime, global multi-region backups, and bank-grade data encryption.

3. Dedicated VPS Cloud Server (Hosting the Trouble Cheat Backend Engine)
Why it is needed: The mobile app on the phone is only the user-facing interface. The central "brain" of the platform is the backend server, which calculates risk scores, tracks active countdown timers, enforces subscription rules, and commands Telnyx to make emergency calls.
What it does: A dedicated Virtual Private Server (VPS) runs this backend software 24 hours a day, 365 days a year on a high-speed cloud connection with an encrypted internet address (HTTPS). Even if a user's phone runs out of battery, the VPS continues tracking the timer and will automatically alert emergency contacts if the user fails to check in.

4. Public Live Tracking Web Portal (track.troublecheat.com)
Why it is needed: When an emergency contact receives an emergency text message from Telnyx, they need to view the user's location instantly.
What it does: The web link opens a secure moving map in any web browser on iPhone, Android, Mac, or PC. The contact does not need to download the Trouble Cheat app or create an account; they can immediately view the live moving pin, incident timeline, and emergency notes.

5. App Store In-App Purchases (Apple Pay & Google Play Billing)
Why it is needed: To allow users to upgrade from the Free tier to Standard or Premium smoothly.
What it does: Handles monthly and annual subscription billing securely through the user's existing Apple ID or Google Play account with one tap.

5. Critical Deployment Reality: Background Audio & Store Guidelines

A primary requirement in the platform design is enabling users to trigger an emergency simply by speaking their confidential phrase (for example: "Red Umbrella"). It is critical to understand the operating system rules governing this feature on modern smartphones.

The Operating System Restriction: Neither Apple (iOS) nor Google (Android) permits any third-party application to keep a smartphone microphone running 24 hours a day in the background while the phone is locked. Doing so directly violates Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1 (User Privacy) and Google Play Background Execution Limits, which causes immediate store rejection. Furthermore, continuous background audio analysis drains a smartphone battery completely within two to three hours.

The Approved, Highly Effective Solution for Trouble Cheat

To achieve seamless, hands-free protection while guaranteeing 100% compliance and approval on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, Trouble Cheat utilizes three approved mechanisms:

1. Siri and Google Assistant Voice Integration (Primary Hands-Free Trigger)
The platform connects directly into Apple’s native Siri Shortcuts and Google’s Assistant App Actions. Modern smartphones already contain a dedicated low-power microchip that listens exclusively for the native assistant prompt. The user simply says:

"Hey Siri, Red Umbrella"

Even if the phone is locked, placed in a pocket, or resting on a table, the operating system instantly recognizes the command, awakens Trouble Cheat in the background, and dispatches the emergency SOS without requiring the user to touch the screen.

2. Active Session Listening (During Walking and Monitoring Modes)
Operating system guidelines explicitly allow continuous background microphone listening when an active security, navigation, or workout session is in progress. When the user starts a "Safety Timer" or activates the "Safety Monitor", the app enters an active security session and can listen for the safe phrase continuously in the background.

3. Stealth Hardware Button Triggers (Pocket Activation)
In situations where speaking aloud could escalate danger (such as an aggressive confrontation or a tense traffic stop), speaking a verbal phrase is inadvisable. The platform supports physical hardware triggers:

  • On iPhone: Tapping the back of the phone twice (iOS Back Tap) or holding designated volume buttons triggers the silent alert.
  • On Android: Rapidly pressing the side power button three times dispatches the stealth distress signal.
The user can call for help with their hand resting inside their pocket without saying a single word.
